Skip new objects whose prerequisite the policy withholds and the target lacks, instead of throwing a missing-requirement error or emitting DDL that fails at apply. A user trigger on a reference-only `supabase_migrations.schema_migrations` (CLI-2300), pgsodium column defaults, views and security labels (CLI-2342), and views over a Wrappers foreign table (CLI-2178) are now reverted at plan time into `filteredDeltas` with their subtree and reported as `excluded-by-cascade` warnings in `plan.diagnostics` (blocking under `--strict-coverage`). A stranded change to an existing object, or a new constraint or RESTRICTIVE RLS policy (unless its table is itself missing), is refused with a plan-time error instead, because skipping it would leave the old definition, missing enforcement or wider access live. Prerequisites that exist on the target, are produced by the plan, or are platform-provisioned still plan as before. The Supabase system-extension, system-schema and system-role-owner exclusion rules now carry readable reason codes.
